The road to the platinum

PowerPyx rates this set 4/10 and about 200 hours; Push Square warns it can run past 300. One playthrough is enough, everything is offline, and there are no difficulty trophies. Work the three phases in order.

Phase 1

Play the story 60–80 hours

Finish Chapters 1–5 and enjoy it — the story trophies pop on their own. While you play, scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals through the binoculars; both count toward later trophies. Do optional missions as they appear: many unlock features the trophies need.

  • Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation — the only thing in the game you can miss (see below).
  • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. There is no mission replay and no New Game+.
  • Make a save before the final mission.

Phase 2

The 100% checklist ~100 hours

After the story, the checklist appears under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% true completion: most side quests are not required and most collectibles are only partly required.

  • Scan any 200 of 277 vehicles; study any 100 of 255 animals.
  • Complete any 70 of 80 challenges; 300 stunts; 100 optional missions.
  • All robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests.

Phase 3

Cleanup ~10 hours

Strangers & Freaks strands are not needed for 100%, so finish the eight that count, then sweep up whatever miscellaneous trophies are left — the partner robberies, the tank, the plane, the showroom Banshee and the money trophies.

  • Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands.
  • Check this tracker for anything unmarked and work it in any order.
  • The platinum pops the moment the 54th other trophy does.

⚠ The one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot. One meditation exists only in Chapter 1, on the balcony of Jason’s house: after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her out and choose Accept. It is still unconfirmed whether the trophy actually requires it, but it costs a minute and it is the only risk in the whole list. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story.

Cheats switch trophies off

Using any cheat disables trophies and saving. Keep cheats off for the whole run — and note that cheats only unlock after the story anyway.

Save like it matters

15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep one save at the start and one at the end of each chapter (10 slots), and rotate the remaining 5 for free-roam cleanup.
