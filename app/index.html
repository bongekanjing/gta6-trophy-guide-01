Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough. PowerPyx rates the platinum 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours; there are no online trophies and no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off.

    The one trophy you can miss

    Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only trophy PowerPyx flags as possibly missable: during Chapter 1, one morning at Jason’s house, Lucia meditates on the balcony. Follow her and accept the optional quest — that meditation is gone after Chapter 1. Everything else can be finished after the story.
